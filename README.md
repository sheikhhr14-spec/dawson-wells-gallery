# Dawson Wells Gallery

We are seeking to design and develop a modern, sophisticated, professional, and scalable website that will represent Dawson Wells Gallery on a global level, effectively showcase our collection, and support the continued growth of our startup.
Dawson Wells Gallery specializes in high-quality Modern and Contemporary Art, with a strong emphasis on presentation, visual storytelling, and delivering a comprehensive and engaging experience for visitors.
Website Reference & Vision
As a reference point, you may review the following benchmark website:  https://www.guidomauas.com/.
While this website provides a useful point of reference, we are looking for a more refined, visually compelling, modern, and high-performing experience that reflects the identity and positioning of Dawson Wells Gallery.
The website will function primarily as an online showcase and digital representation of the gallery rather than an e-commerce platform. We do not intend to sell artworks directly through the website. Instead, the primary objective is to showcase our collection, generate interest, and encourage visitors to engage with and ultimately visit the physical gallery. At launch, we anticipate approximately 100 artworks, providing a strong and well-rounded foundation for the website.
Artwork Presentation & Search
Each artwork should have its own dedicated detail page featuring:
Multiple high-resolution images
Artwork title
Dimensions
Medium
Year
Additional artwork information where applicable
The website should also include a search and filtering system that allows visitors to easily browse and discover artworks by category, including:
Photography
Paintings
Drawings
Ceramics
Prints
We do not intend to feature multiple artists at this stage, as all artworks presented on the website will be exclusively from the Dawson Wells Gallery collection.
Proposed Website Structure
The proposed website structure should include:
Home
About
Mission & Vision
Company Overview
Artworks
Photography
Paintings
Drawings
Ceramics
Prints
News & Updates
Contact
Inquiry/Contact Form
The primary navigation menu and any associated sub-menus should maintain a consistent design across all pages. The News & Updates section should function as a blog-style platform where we can publish announcements, upcoming events, gallery activities, new artwork additions, and other relevant updates.
Administrative & Content Management
From an administrative perspective, we require a user-friendly backend/CMS that allows our team to efficiently manage:
Website content and pages
Artwork listings and artwork information
Images and media
News and updates
Contact/inquiry submissions
Automated email notifications following each inquiry form submission
Website theme and appearance settings
General website configuration
The CMS should be simple enough for our team to make routine updates without requiring ongoing developer assistance.
Project Scope
The proposed project scope should include:
Wireframing and information architecture
UI/UX and frontend design
Backend development and CMS implementation
Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile
Artwork catalog and filtering functionality
Contact/inquiry form integration
Project management
Testing and quality assurance
Performance optimization
Final deployment and launch
Additional Requirements
Please also include the following within your proposal:
Website developed in English language only
Domain registration/management
Web hosting
SSL/security configuration
Ongoing monthly maintenance and technical support
Website updates and performance monitoring
Post-launch SEO services to improve the gallery's visibility and organic search presence
Any necessary third-party integrations or services required for the website to operate effectively
We will provide all necessary content, branding materials, logo, written copy, artwork information, images, and other  assets required for the project.
